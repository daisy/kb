Explanation

Code 76 indicates that a publication is exempt from the accessibility requirements of the European Accessibility Act (EAA) under the dispropotionate burden exception.

Justification of the exemption can be included in an <ProductFormFeatureDescription> element.

Only for use in ONIX 3.0 or later.
